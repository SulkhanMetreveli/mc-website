About

Met Capital employs quantitative and computational methods in the design and execution of its investment programmes. We combine systematic modelling, machine learning, and rigorous empirical analysis to identify opportunities across global markets.

Our research foundation distinguishes our approach. Through the ACR Institute, we conduct original inquiry into artificial intelligence, computational theory, and the mathematical properties of complex systems. These investigations inform our systematic methodologies and our understanding of where algorithmic approaches have decisive advantages—and where they do not.

We operate investment vehicles and maintain private equity positions in technology companies aligned with our research interests. Our holding structure allows us to deploy capital systematically while supporting fundamental research on a long-term basis.

Research Foundation

The ACR Institute is a Swiss non-profit research association conducting inquiry into consciousness, computation, and complex systems. This research informs our systematic investment frameworks.

Leadership

Dr. Sulkhan Metreveli

Founder & Chief Executive Officer
PhD, University of Zurich Director, ACR Institute

Dr. Metreveli founded Met Capital to bridge rigorous research and systematic investment management. His academic work focuses on artificial intelligence, machine learning, and the computational limits of algorithmic systems—research conducted through the ACR Institute, which he established as a Swiss non-profit dedicated to fundamental inquiry.

He holds a doctorate (PhD) in communication science from the University of Zurich.

Account Security

Two-Factor Authentication

Two-factor authentication is required on every Met Capital login. You'll need an authenticator app such as Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Authy, or 1Password.

Two-factor authentication is on Active

Your account asks for a code from your authenticator app when you sign in. Stay signed in on this browser and you won't be asked again until you log out.

Recovery codes

If you lose your device, a recovery code lets you sign in and set up a new authenticator. Each code works once. Generating new codes replaces any older ones.

Store these somewhere safe now. They will not be shown again.

Lost your device and your recovery codes? Contact Met Capital directly — after verifying you by phone, we can reset two-factor authentication on your account.
